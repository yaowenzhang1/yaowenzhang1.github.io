I am an independent researcher. My research focuses on computer vision and multi‐modal learning, particularly in areas of large vision‐language models. In addition to my scientific research, my primary professional role is as a Software Development Engineer at [JD.com](https://ir.jd.com/about-JD), dedicated to the Operation Platform Center (OPC) of [Joybuy](https://www.joybuy.com/).

I received my M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence from Nanyang Technological University (NTU) and my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC).
